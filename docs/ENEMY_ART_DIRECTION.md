# PlaneShift enemy art and animation direction

PlaneShift keeps its familiar registry IDs for save/network compatibility, but the production
visuals are an original sky-island cast. The source turnarounds are under
`tools/art_sources/enemy_turnaround_*.png`; the runtime cuboid skins are generated from the Mew
concept atlas by `tools/MewRigSkinImporter.java`.

## Production model and UV contract

`BespokeEnemyModel` now bakes eleven independent 3D layers from these turnarounds. The profiles
share animation plumbing but not geometry: low enemies have six legs, the torpedo is horizontal,
the manta includes its rider, the plant has separate upper/lower jaw geometry, and the monarch has
horns, jaw, shell mantle, limbs and a two-stage tail. `AnimatedCourseEnemyModel` remains only for
the non-hostile shopkeeper until its dedicated pass.

Enemy sheets are 128x128 and are generated by `tools/EnemyTextureGen.java` from the original
`tools/art_sources/enemy_material_atlas.png`. Broad material families live at `(0,0)`, `(64,0)`,
`(0,40)`, `(64,40)`, `(0,80)` and `(64,80)`. The generator owns exact cube UVs and paints identity
on both lateral faces as well as the front, because 2.5D gameplay sees a travel-facing enemy from
the side. Do not replace these with a front-only character skin.

Procedural animation drives walk, idle, wing flap, sprout bounce, crusher slam, wisp fringe,
six-leg crawler gaits, plant bite and boss weight/tail motion. Runtime remains dependency-free;
Blockbench is still useful for previews and future hand-keyed clip design.
